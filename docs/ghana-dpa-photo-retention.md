# Ghana Data Protection & photo retention (thin)

Operational summary for LTJBM CDMS operators. **Not legal advice.**

In-app copy: Data Centre → **Data protection** (`/admin/compliance`).

## What we store

- Membership biodata, household links, sacraments, photos (`photoKey` / gated `/uploads`)
- Attendance, events/room bookings, pastoral notes (restricted)
- Head Office contribution obligations and payments

## Access rules already enforced

- RBAC by role; Board / Leadership Council = **aggregates only** on giving
- Pastoral notes restricted; Member portal = own profile + own statement
- Audit log browser for Admin (`/admin/audit`)

## Photo retention (recommended)

1. Keep photos while the member is Active / Visitor or identification is needed for follow-up.
2. After Transfer, Deceased, or long Inactive: remove or archive photos within a documented window (**24 months** recommended default unless a live pastoral/legal need remains).
3. Treat backups/exports with the same sensitivity.

## Member rights (operational)

Route view / correct / erase requests through Data Centre. Financial history may have limited erasure where accountability or statutory retention applies — document any refusal.

Engage counsel for formal registration with Ghana’s Data Protection Commission / DPIA as needed.
